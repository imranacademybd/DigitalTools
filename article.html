How to Build a Simple SEO Plan for a New Website

September 16, 2026 · 6 min read

A new website does not need a huge SEO plan on day one. Start with the basics, publish useful pages, and improve them over time.

1. Choose clear topics

Start by listing the questions your readers ask. Group similar questions into topics. This helps you create pages that have a clear purpose.

2. Research keywords

Find the words people use when they search. Look for useful phrases that match your topic and your audience. Do not force keywords into every sentence.

3. Create helpful content

Write for the reader first. Use a clear title, short paragraphs, useful headings, examples and direct answers. Make each page better than a basic definition.

4. Improve technical SEO

  • Make the site mobile friendly.
  • Use fast, reliable hosting.
  • Add descriptive page titles and meta descriptions.
  • Use clean URLs.
  • Check broken links and indexing issues.

5. Add internal links

Link related articles together. A visitor reading one guide should be able to discover the next useful guide with a natural link.

6. Track and improve

SEO takes time. Watch your search performance, identify pages that need work, and update content when the information changes.

Simple rule: Make every page useful enough that a real reader would want to save or share it.
